Welcome to Tartan & Thistle

Tartan & Thistle Spa & Boutique was born from a long-held vision, a dream nurtured over the past nine years and brought to life through passion, dedication and experience. During this time, I successfully founded and operated a reputable Dog Daycare and Grooming Salon, gaining invaluable insight into canine care and client service.

With that foundation, I turned my focus toward realising the unique concept of Tartan & Thistle Spa & Boutique, while also advancing my professional journey as both a Pet Aesthetician and continuing working towards my Master Groomer Certification. At Tartan & Thistle Spa & Boutique, my mission extends beyond exceptional grooming.

I am deeply committed to positive grooming experiences for both dogs and owners; the art and science of canine skincare, offering specialised aesthetician services for dogs experiencing skin or coat challenges and for those whose owners wish to preserve and enhance their pet’s natural health and well-being.

I look forward to meeting and getting to know you and your dogs!

Rebekah

Sizing Guide

Why We Use a Sizing Guide:

At Tartan & Thistle, we know no two dogs are alike. Their size, coat, temperament and style preferences all contribute to the time and care they need. That's why we've moved away from generic breed-based pricing and created our own fair and thoughtful sizing guide, ensuring every dog receives personalised care and every owner understands what to expect.

Our Hourly Rate:

We charge based on an hourly grooming rate. Your final price reflects the time your dog's groom takes, based on their size, coat type and service needs, not their breed name alone. This allows for fair pricing and complete transparency while ensuring our team can deliver a calm, unhurried grooming experience every time.
