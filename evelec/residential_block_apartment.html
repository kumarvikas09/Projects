EV chargepoints for residential blocks and apartments

If you are an owner or property manager of a residential apartment, you will want to ensure your properties have the very best facilities and are energy efficient. There are various grants available for the installation of charge points within carparks of residential buildings and apartment blocks. Including grants for charge points only and those for the main infrastructure to enable the wider installation of charge points on a larger building. Electric vehicle chargepoints for landlords and managing agents is a perfect solution to explore as they can be managed with sophisticated back office systems to allow for easy billing and costing.

Grants for residential block charging

The OZEV (EV Charge Point) Grant is available for the installation of charge points within carparks of residential buildings and apartment blocks.

To be eligible for the grant, the following criteria applies:

  • Designated off road parking space/s
  • Eligible car – either on order or in possession (company cars are eligible)
  • Approval from landlord or managing agent
