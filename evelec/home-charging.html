Charging For Home

If you have ordered your electric vehicle (or are thinking of making the switch and are researching your options) how you will charge your new car efficiently at home is going to be top of your list.

Residents of rental properties or owners of flats/apartments with designated parking spaces are eligible for the new OZEV EV Charge Point Grant. This entitles the owner up to £350 off the cost of the EV Charge Point (subject to eligibility).

Electric Vehicle Charging at Home

EV charging at home is an important feature for electric vehicle owners, and it is estimated that over 80% of all charging takes place at home.

As well as being OZEV-approved home chargepoint installers, we are approved partners with leading manufacturers Andersen, Easee, Hypervolt, Simpson & Partners and zappi.

This means we can offer a full range of chargepoint units for your needs, with different designs and options available for you to choose from.
