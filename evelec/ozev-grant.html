OZEV grant

As approved OZEV installers, we can supply and install the chargepoint of your choice.

We also handle all of the paperwork on your behalf.

At home

Residents of rental properties or owners of flats/apartments with designated parking spaces are now eligible for the new OZEV EV Charge Point Grant.

This entitles the owner up to £350 off the cost of the EV Charge Point (subject to meeting all eligibility criteria).

At work

The OZEV Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) grant enables any home or business to receive a £350 grant per socket to install EV charge points.

It is capped at £350 per socket, up to a maximum of 20 sockets, and can be used for commercial premises serving staff and customers/visitors – or both.
