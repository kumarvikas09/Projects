Electrical Services

In addition to our electric vehicle installation options, we offer a range of general electrical services. Our team of electricians in Brighton look after residential and commercial customers in Sussex and beyond.

All works completed by our team will be signed-off upon completion, with correct certification issued within 28 days.

01

Emergency call-outs

If you need an engineer with you urgently, to diagnose or fix an electrical issue, we have a team locally who can arrange to be with you swiftly. Our call-out rates are some of the most competitive around.

02

Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICR)

Ensure your property is as safe as can be, with a full condition report highlighting all safety risks as set out by BS 7671.

03

Fault Finding

We can fix and repair what isn’t working, quickly and cost effectively.

04

Full and Partial Rewire

Whether a full property rewire or partial rewire for a kitchen or loft extension, our team will work efficiently and effectively alongside other tradespeople.

05

New Fuse Boards

Adopt the new 18th Edition safety standards by installing a new RCD protected, Metal Clad consumer units for increased safety.

06

Energy Saving Solutions

From LED lighting to new energy efficient heating products, energy reduction is a high priority for all households and consumers looking to have a positive impact on the environment as well as saving money.

07

Garden Lighting and Socket Installations

Make the garden an extra room by adding an atmospheric lighting system or weather proof sockets.

08

Extra Sockets

Add extra sockets to an existing installation and reduce the risk of fire by removing the need for extension leads.

09

New Lighting Installations

From a new light fitting in your dining room to a full installation of LED kitchen downlights, we can advise you on the best scheme and fittings for your space as well as installation.

10

Nest Technology

We are Nest Pro Installers, which means we are trained to install Nest products – in the home or commercial premises.

11

Portable Appliance Testing

Keep your employees safe and insurers happy by maintaining and documenting appliance care.
