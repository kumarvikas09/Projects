Who We Are

Your Trusted Coal Trading Partner

At SVP Minerals, we bridge high-capacity international mining origins and domestic industrial powerhouses. With established trading desks headquartered in Gujarat, India, we specialize in the procurement, quality calibration, and multimodal distribution of solid fuels.

Our core commodities span Indonesian imported thermal coal (GAR 3600 to 5000), domestic Indian coalfields, and commercial-grade lignite. By combining deep maritime logistics management with direct producer partnerships, we provide end-to-end predictability for thermal power plants, cement producers, and industrial boiler operators.

  • Direct allocations from premier Indonesian mines: MIFA, BSSR, ATM, LANA, KUTAI & SINGLURUS
  • Port stockyard management with integrated vessel discharge, railway rakes, and road transport
  • Certified calorific testing (GAR/NAR), moisture control, and transparent commercial contracts
