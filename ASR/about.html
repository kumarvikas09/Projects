About ASR Logistics

A medical courier built from the ground up to serve the healthcare community with speed, care, and integrity.

OUR MISSION

We Carry More Than Packages

ASR Logistics was founded with one clear mission: give healthcare providers and pharmacies a courier service they can depend on without compromise. We recognize that what we carry isn't just everyday freight — it's someone's health, urgent therapy, or critical diagnostic results.

Unlike generic delivery providers, we partner exclusively with pharmacies, outpatient clinics, testing laboratories, and medical centers across Saskatoon. Our couriers are trained in chain-of-custody documentation, temperature integrity, and direct signature confirmation.

  • Direct-to-patient prescription deliveries with signature confirmation
  • Temperature-sensitive vaccine, biological, and specimen transport
  • Inter-clinic supply runs, emergency transfers, and medical document custody
