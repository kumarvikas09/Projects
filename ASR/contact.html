FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our healthcare courier solutions, service coverage, scheduling, and handling protocols in Saskatoon.

We currently serve the greater Saskatoon area, connecting community pharmacies, clinics, medical offices, diagnostic laboratories, and patient residences. If you have delivery requirements outside Saskatoon or for a specific healthcare facility, contact us and we will confirm routing availability.

We provide both scheduled recurring routes and on-demand runs. Many pharmacies and clinics use our recurring routes for predictable daily batches, while relying on our on-demand services for urgent, stat, or emergency deliveries when unexpected needs arise.

We specialize in five core medical transport services: Prescription Delivery (direct to patient residences or care homes), Clinic Deliveries (inter-office supply and equipment runs), Lab & Specimen Runs (swabs, blood draws, and pathology specimens), Vaccine Delivery (strict cold-chain management), and Document Transport (confidential records, referrals, and authorizations).

Every delivery is handled with proper chain-of-custody documentation and strict privacy protocols. Temperature-sensitive items such as biological specimens and vaccines are transported using dedicated insulated containers and temperature-controlled monitoring, while confidential healthcare documents remain sealed, tracked, and tamper-evident.

Getting started is simple. You can submit our contact form above or call our local team directly at (639) 471-3662. We’ll discuss your pickup locations, delivery frequency, and any specialized handling protocols, then establish an account and route tailored to your facility.

Every run is documented from pickup to drop-off. We provide delivery confirmation records, including physical signature capture on request for prescriptions and confidential documents, giving your facility complete visibility and verified proof of delivery.
