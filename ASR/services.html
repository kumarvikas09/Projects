  • Signature confirmation available

  • Same-day and scheduled runs

Lab & Specimen Runs

Specimen integrity is non-negotiable. We provide chain-of-custody documentation and temperature-controlled transport for biological samples, swabs, blood draws, and pathology specimens.

We work directly with labs, pathology centers, and diagnostic facilities to ensure samples arrive in optimal condition and within required timeframes.

  • Chain-of-custody documentation
  • Temperature-controlled transport

  • Time-critical scheduling
