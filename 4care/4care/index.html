Who are we?

Empowering Independence Through Compassionate Care

At 4Care, we are committed to helping people with vision impairment live with confidence, dignity, and independence. Through personalised NDIS support, our caring team works alongside participants to achieve their goals, build everyday skills, and create meaningful opportunities for a more independent life.

Our Mission

To empower people with vision impairment through compassionate, person-centred NDIS support that promotes independence, confidence, and meaningful participation in everyday life. We are committed to delivering personalised services that help every individual achieve their goals and live life on their own terms.

Our Vision

To create an inclusive community where people with vision impairment have equal opportunities to thrive, connect, and live independently. We envision a future where every individual is supported with dignity, respect, and the confidence to reach their full potential.

How we work

Helping You Build Confidence to Navigate Life Independently

  • We help you understand your NDIS plan and how to get the most from it.
  • We support applications for assistive technology, home and living options, and more.
  • We educate and champion your power of choice and control over your supports.
  • We source and connect you with trusted NDIS and mainstream providers.
  • We break down confusing NDIS processes into plain, practical steps.
  • We help you manage your NDIS budget flexibly and with confidence.
  • We liaise with NDIS and mainstream services so support is delivered without gaps.
  • Most importantly — we listen to what you want to achieve, and help build the plan to get you there.
