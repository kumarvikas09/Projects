NDIS Support Coordination Services to Meet Your Goals

Connecting you with the right providers, building your independence, and guiding you through every step of your NDIS journey.

The NDIS can be a daunting experience. Thankfully our experienced & dedicated Support Coordinators & Specialist Support Coordinators have the experience to make navigating the NDIS a smoother experience for our clients.

About Support Coordination

Support Coordination That Meets Your Goals

Our experienced Support Coordinators will assist you, your family and your representatives with what services are available for you to be able to make informed decisions.

They will support you to choose how you want your services to be provided and who you want to provide them. They can help you find the right providers for you, and get those services set up – including supporting you to decide the budget for each type of support plus negotiating prices and service agreements.

How We Can Assist With Your NDIS Plan:

Inform you about the different possible things you can do with your NDIS funds
Fully exercise choice and control
Comply through NDIS marketplace and choose the best providers that suit you, your goals and location
Adequately implement your NDIS plan
Better manage/direct your own supports
Have more opportunities to explore and connect with community and alternative support
Identify options (funded, mainstream and informal networks)
Investigate options
Understand funding flexibility
Reach decisions regarding services
Reach agreement with providers
Commence service and ensure new support arrangements thrive
Ensure you get value for money from your plan
Assist in monitoring your NDIS budget and funding expenditure
Specialist Coordination Services for resolving points of crisis, parenting training and helping you integrate into your network or community
